## Design
Synthetic state x obeys dx/dt=a(|j|)(1-x)-0.06*x^3, x(0)=0, shared known observation map from v0.8.5. Two true rates: rational 0.1*j^2/(1+0.7*j^2), exponential 0.12*(1-exp(-j^2/0.9)). Both are illustrative saturation laws, not experimentally identified. Four training profiles of six 6-hour blocks (148 voltage readings including rest), current magnitude 0,.25,.75,1.5,3, sign reversals and rests. Test profiles held out entirely: two interpolation profiles with nonzero magnitudes .5,1,2; two mixed profiles reaching 4 (includes both in-domain and OOD amplitudes; RMSE across entire profiles); two history profiles sharing current after different initial pulse histories. No test voltage or latent labels used for fitting. Each reference step uses solve_ivp with tight tolerances and constant j within the interval; switches at exact integer-hour boundaries. Fitting/rollout uses RK4 dt=.5 h. Maximum RK4 state RMSE vs reference 9.259e-8 and 7.487e-8 for respective truths. All evaluated states in [0,.882197]. Known sign symmetry, gain, decay coefficient, relaxation baseline, and BV j0 are provided to every model.

Methods: stateless PCHIP assumes constant current since time zero (negative control); misspecified dynamic quadratic; four free nonnegative nodal rates with PCHIP and constant extension beyond 3; oracle knows correct two-parameter family but fits coefficients; neural ODE uses three tanh units and softplus positive output with bounded transformed current, all 10 parameters fitted by finite-difference least squares through RK4, fixed .15 L2 residual toward initialization. This is a solver-integrated neural constitutive law, not a dynamic PINN with collocation residual; matched dynamic PINN deferred. PCHIP shape-preserving does not imply fitted nodes constrained monotonic. Both flexible models have different inductive biases and parameter counts. Neural seeds coupled to noise seeds; no hyperparameter sweep or independent optimizer/noise factorial.

## Findings
See [group means](results/v086_dynamic_summary.csv). Interpolation mean over two truths and three seeds at noise 1/5 mV: stateless 3.192/3.318, misspecified 5.510/5.446, PCHIP .219/.683, oracle .095/.453, neural ODE .306/1.877. Mixed extrapolation profiles at 1 mV: PCHIP .727, neural .602, oracle .211; at 5 mV PCHIP 1.721, neural 3.618, oracle .959. Neural train errors close to PCHIP, poorer interpolation at higher noise suggests weakly constrained between-level rate shape, not proof of general neural disadvantage. History pair at 24h, j=.75: reference voltage differences 7.5315 and 6.4257 mV for rational and exponential truth. Stateless exactly zero; PCHIP means 7.7725/6.6574 at 1 mV, neural 7.7652/6.6552; at 5 mV PCHIP 8.2838/7.2960, neural 8.2721/7.2844. Physics-based state memory is sufficient to capture the synthetic history effect; neural learning is not required. Rest voltage is zero irrespective of x by construction, so rest-state prediction is not directly observed.

Limitations: two synthetic rate laws, three seeds, known observation map and one latent state, no actual battery data, no independent measurement of x, no statistical superiority claim. No transient switching artifacts or coupled transport. Next: matched segment-wise dynamic PINN, separate noise/initialization seeds, physically constrained rate regularization and training-only model selection, more truths including non-saturation and structural mismatch. Do not infer full P2D validity.
