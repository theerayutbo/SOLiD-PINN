## v0.9.0f: Active Physics Discovery (proposed; not yet executed)
**Research question:** Can experiment design and uncertainty-aware model discrimination resolve the kinetic/terminal-series ambiguity exposed in v0.9.0e, rather than merely improve terminal voltage RMSE?

1. **Sensitivity and local identifiability.** Build scaled central-difference Jacobians of voltage with respect to baseline rates, diffusion, kinetic multiplier and series resistance; whiten by the noise covariance. Verify derivative stability under step-size and mesh refinement; report singular spectrum, smallest/largest singular ratio, near-null parameter directions and correlations. Separate structural from practical identifiability; local SVD cannot prove global uniqueness.
2. **Joint estimation and uncertainty.** Fit kinetic and series simultaneously, include diffusion and recovery as nuisance/alternative hypotheses. Compare bounded multistart LS and flexible closure; inspect profile likelihood (reoptimize nuisance parameters) and paired bootstrap/parametric simulation. Report intervals, boundary hits, prediction bands and internal field errors, not just point estimates. If profiles are flat or candidates indistinguishable, return an ambiguous set rather than force a label.
3. **Prospective experiment design.** Compare existing protocol with matched-budget short/long pulses, multiple amplitudes, polarity reversal, rest and recovery probes; optimize whitened Fisher information (E-optimal smallest eigenvalue and D-optimal log determinant, with parameter scaling and prior/regularization stated) plus minimum pairwise predicted separation. Constrain total time, sampled points, charge throughput, normalized concentration bounds and current range. Choose designs using training/design simulations only, lock before independent evaluation. If electrical data remain insufficient, add independent frequency-resolved EIS or geometry/transport measurements; do not fabricate observations.
4. **Robustness and sample size.** Repeat with fresh calibration and held-out seeds, varied mechanism strengths, mixed mechanisms and unknown/misspecified covariance (estimate on training data only). Suggested at least 100 independent null and 30 alternative draws per principal regime, report exact binomial intervals and failure counts; size final confirmatory test for a prespecified upper-confidence criterion. Use separate independent profiles and simulation families, and compare null-calibrated gate versus abstention/model-set approach. No threshold retuning on final test. Report false activation and family confusion, prediction, internal flux accuracy and interval coverage. Known-covariance baseline retained.
5. **Acceptance gates (proposed, not results).** Recheck conservation, sampled bounds/passivity, mesh/time convergence <=1 mV on tested profiles; report every failed fit. Compare matched-budget designs using locked held-out seeds; require evidence that kinetic/series discrimination or calibrated ambiguity reporting improves without unacceptable false activation or loss of prediction quality. A 10% target is not certified unless the chosen confidence criterion is met. Failure means retain ambiguity and redesign measurements, not claim discovery.
6. **v0.9.1 experimental bridge.** Obtain current/time/cycle metadata, geometry, independent transport/kinetics constraints and OCV mapping; separate cell chemistries and reserve independent cells/protocols for final test. Fit simple physics first and compare joint/learned candidates under matched data budgets. Do not transfer symmetric-cell ASR to a cathode or publish collaborator traces. Full nonlinear P2D, independently verified solver and experimental validation remain distinct gates before v1.0.0 uncertainty-aware Hybrid PINN.
